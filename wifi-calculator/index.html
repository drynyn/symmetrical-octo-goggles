Early prototype · indoor coverage planning

Estimate how walls and distance may affect a Wi-Fi connection. This version uses configurable illustrative assumptions, not measured Sky hub performance.

The reference signals, distance-loss exponent, wall losses, floor losses and quality thresholds are unvalidated placeholders. They are not Sky specifications or guaranteed predictions.

Important limitation

This simple model cannot account properly for wall thickness and angle, insulation, furniture, interference, antenna orientation, client device, channel width, or the hub's band-steering behaviour. It estimates a broad signal range; it does not guarantee speed or reliability. Use measurements from real homes to calibrate it before relying on the result for customer advice.
