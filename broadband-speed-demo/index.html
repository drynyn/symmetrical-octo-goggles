About property data: this keyless version validates the postcode and builds the address from the details you enter. It does not automatically retrieve floor area or room count. Those fields are optional and must be entered from a reliable source, such as the property’s Energy Performance Certificate (EPC). The number of habitable rooms on an EPC may differ from the total number of rooms.

This is an early demonstration, not a broadband availability checker. The estimate is a planning guide, not a guarantee of performance or a confirmation that a particular speed is available at the address.
